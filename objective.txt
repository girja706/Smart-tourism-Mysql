
                                                                                Project Objectives

                                                                     Smart Tourism & Travel Analytics System


The Smart Tourism & Travel Analytics System is a MySQL-based project designed to manage tourist information, destinations, hotels, travel packages, bookings, payments, reviews and transport details. The system helps in providing a centralized platform to store, retrieve and analyze travel-related data, enabling better decision-making and improved travel experiences.
Objectives


1. Manage Tourist Information
Store and maintain details of tourists such as name, gender, age, email, city and registration date.

2.Manage Destinations
Keep information about tourist destinations including location, state, country, category and best season.

3.Manage Hotels
Store hotel details like name, type, price per night, rating and availability for each destination.

4.Manage Travel Packages
Maintain package details such as name, destination, duration, price and inclusions.

5.Handle Bookings
Allow tourists to book travel packages, hotels and transport, and track booking status, dates and total amount.

6.Manage Payments
Record payment details including amount, method, date and status.

7.Manage Reviews
Collect and analyze tourist reviews and ratings for destinations and hotels.

8. Manage Transport
Store transport details such as mode, operator, cost and booking information.

Provide Analytics
Use SQL queries, joins, stored procedures, functions and aggregate operations to generate useful travel analytics reports (e.g, top destinations, popular hotels, booking trends, revenue analysis).

Expected Outcome
The project will result in a well-structured database with 8 related tables, complete SQL operations (basic queries, joins, stored procedures, functions), and meaningful travel analytics to support data-driven decision making in the tourism industry .
